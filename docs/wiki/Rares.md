Rares uses the Kills switch. When you kill a rare or rare elite, Dossier stores the zone, map coordinates, the character, the time, and the loot you actually received. It does not include a drop list from outside the game.

The last 20 places you killed that rare stay on its row. Track latest adds a one-time task for the newest rare. If nothing has dropped yet, the row says "No drops recorded yet."
