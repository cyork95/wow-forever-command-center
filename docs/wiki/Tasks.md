Tasks are notes you write: daily, weekly, or once. A task can name a zone. Turning in a quest with the same name, or killing a matching rare, marks it done. Daily tasks wait for the quest reset. Weekly tasks wait for the weekly reset, or seven days when the game does not say.

`/dossier tasks` opens the Tasks tab and a window for this zone. The window lists open tasks, shopping items you are short of, rares recorded in the zone, and how many completed quests belong to the zone. It can open when you change zones, stays closed in combat, and stays dismissed until the next zone change. The switch on the window turns that off.

The shopping list itself stays on the Shopping tab.
