The Ledger switch records gold, currencies, and reputation for the character you are playing. Currencies and Reputation are the same switch. Other characters on the account show up from the account-wide save, and only the character you are on is written.

Gold is grouped by where it came from: Auction House, Vendors, Questing, Looting, Mail, Trades, and Other. The Auction House view includes a rough 30-day sale rate when the game says a listing sold or expired.

The Session tab adds one line for this session, such as `Ledger: Vendors -20s, Honor +12`.
