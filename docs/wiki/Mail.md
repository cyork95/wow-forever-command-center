Mail stores letters you send and receive: who, subject, body, gold, items, and whether the letter is waiting, taken, returned, or deleted. Search, and filter Received, Sent, or All.

Dossier does not send, take, return, or open mail. On login it can print a line when a stored letter is close to expiring.

If Parcel already saved a recognizable letter list, Dossier copies it once.
