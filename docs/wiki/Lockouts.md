Lockouts has its own switch. It stores the instances you are saved to, and a diary of the last 50 runs: when you entered and left, your level, gold, how long it took, and mobs when Kills is on.

The first time Dossier has no runs of its own, it can copy runs from Nova Instance Tracker. After Dossier has a run or a lockout, Nova's block stays out of the export.

Sessions, under the Session switch, lists finished play sessions for every character. The Session tab is still the live timer.
