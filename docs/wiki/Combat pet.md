# Combat pet

Dossier records the combat pet this character is using, and the pets in the stable. That is the hunter beast, the warlock demon, or any other pet the game puts on the pet unit. Battle pets in the pet journal stay in Collections.

## The export

The Combat pet section is on the Character card and is ticked by default.

- **Status** is `Out` or `Dismissed`. Dismissed means the last pet that was out, kept so an export still names it after you put it away.
- **Name, family, type, level, health, diet, happiness, and abilities** appear when the game provides them. A wolf's family is Wolf. An imp's family is the demon type.
- **Stable** lists each stabled pet with family and level. Those are the pets this character can call instead.

`No combat pet.` means this character has none out and none saved.

## Turning it off

`/dossier off pet` stops watching pet changes and greys the section out of the export. The last saved pet stays. `/dossier on pet` brings it back. You can also leave the section unticked on one export without turning the feature off. See [[Options]].
