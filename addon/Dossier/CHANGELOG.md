# Changelog

## 2.2.8

- The Professions tab is gone. Dossier no longer records skill ranks. Saved rows stay. Altoholic still shows them on the site, and opening a profession window still fills the recipe section of an export.
- The window is shorter, and each tab sits inset from both sides of the rail so the labels that remain fit.
- Options lists the switches that are still in the addon: Biography, Kills, Session, Mail, Lockouts, Tasks, Quests, Shopping list, Screenshotter, and Other addons.
